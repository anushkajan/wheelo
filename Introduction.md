# WHEELO
A vehicle booking system that enables online bookings, real-time vehicle availability, dynamic pricing, and efficient management of different vehicle categories and rental options (self-drive or with driver).

### Pricing/Rate Calculation Logic :
Demonstrated in FigJam file
<br> https://www.figma.com/board/qTaYYAQEoCBTTlAGa4A15i/Vehicle-Rental?node-id=0-1&t=YuEIMYmH6dTyVj7n-1

### What will be booked/reserved :
2-wheeler and 4-wheeler motor vehicles for Self-Driving and With a Driver.
<br>Vehicles are categorised as Luxury and Non-Luxury for now
<br>All will be registered under commercial or rent-a-motor vehicle registration

### Target Users : 
College Students from Other States, Tourists, Residents of State with no vehicle.
